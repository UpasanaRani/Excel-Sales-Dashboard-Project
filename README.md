📊 Excel Sales Dashboard – Sales Performance & Profitability Analysis

📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive Sales Dashboard built using Microsoft Excel to analyze business sales performance, revenue distribution, and profitability.

The dashboard provides an executive-level overview of important business metrics and helps users explore sales trends across different countries, product categories, regions, quarters, and sales channels.

This project was developed by following a step-by-step Excel tutorial as part of my Data Analytics learning journey.

🎯 Key Features

- KPI Cards: Total Revenue, Gross Profit, Gross Profit Margin, Total Units Sold, Total Orders, and Cost of Goods Sold (COGS).
- Monthly Sales Analysis: Tracks monthly revenue and gross profit trends.
- Top 5 Countries: Identifies the highest revenue-generating countries.
- Revenue by Channel: Analyzes revenue contribution from different sales channels.
- Regional Analysis: Compares sales performance across different European regions.
- Geographical Analysis: Displays country-wise revenue using a map chart.
- Interactive Filters: Product Category, Country, and Quarter slicers.

📈 Business Insights

This dashboard helps answer important business questions:

1. What is the total revenue and gross profit?
2. How does revenue change month by month?
3. Which countries generate the highest revenue?
4. Which sales channels contribute the most revenue?
5. How do different regions compare in performance?
6. How does product category affect sales performance?
